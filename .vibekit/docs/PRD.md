## 1. Summary

A personal portfolio site that shows the owner's experience and CV. It is for HR staff and freelance employers.

## 2. Problem & goal

- Problem it solves: give recruiters and freelance clients one place to see experience, projects, and skills.
- Primary goal / focus right now: show the CV and work history to HR and freelance employers.
- Why now / motivation: be found and trusted by hiring contacts.

## 3. Users & use cases

- Primary users: HR staff, recruiters, freelance employers.
- Top use cases:
  1. Read work experience and CV.
  2. Browse projects and tech stack.
  3. Contact the owner (mailto form).

## 4. Success criteria

- What good looks like: basic. The site loads, reads well, and shows the CV and projects.
- How we will know we shipped the right thing: the owner is comfortable sending the link to HR and clients.

## 5. Scope

- In scope (this iteration): SEO first, then other areas as needed.
- Out of scope / non-goals: none stated.

## 6. Constraints & assumptions

- Technical / platform constraints: none stated. Existing stack is Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind. No backend; contact uses mailto.
- Deadlines, budget, compliance, or other constraints: none stated.
- Assumptions to validate: none.

## 7. Focus area (brownfield)

Every area is open. Start with SEO: page metadata, Open Graph, structured data, sitemap, and robots.
